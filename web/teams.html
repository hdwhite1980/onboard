Connect your installed Onboard app

In Onboard → AI Settings, sign in to the same Microsoft account, then create a pairing code for this application. This connects the add-in to your local AI; it does not grant Microsoft access. AI Settings can automatically detect your configured Microsoft tenant environment.

Add specific authorized sources

One per line: mail:messageID, meeting:eventID, chat:chatID, channel:teamID|channelID, file:driveID|itemID, transcript:onlineMeetingID|transcriptID. Appropriate Microsoft permissions are required. No source is fabricated.

Only the configured public-content development policy is available. Unknown, Internal, FCI and CUI are not enabled.
